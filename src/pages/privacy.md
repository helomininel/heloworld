This Privacy Policy describes how [helomininel.github.io/heloworld/](https://helomininel.github.io/heloworld/) ("the Site") handles information. The Site is a personal portfolio and has no accounts, no user-to-user messaging, and no forms that collect or transmit personal data to a third-party service.

## Who runs the Site

The Site is published by Heloisa ("Helo") through GitHub Pages (helomininel). You can reach me via the contact links on the [home page](https://helomininel.github.io/heloworld/#connect) (LinkedIn or GitHub).

## What is collected

- **No cookies or analytics:** compliance with this policy — the Site does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics vendor is currently enabled.
- **No submitted data:** there are no contact forms or comment systems on the Site.
- **Fonts & icon assets:** the Site loads some web fonts and icon assets from public CDNs (for example Google Fonts and Material Symbols). Those providers receive basic technical request data (such as your IP address and user agent) as part of serving the files, governed by their own privacy policies.
- **Embedded content:** the Site does not embed advertising networks or social plugins.

## GitHub Pages

The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). GitHub may automatically collect technical log data (for example IPs, pages requested, and timestamps) to operate and secure the service. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) for details.

## Your rights

Since the Site collects no personal data directly, there is nothing stored or processed by us on your behalf. If you believe personal data about you appears through my content and you want it removed, use the contact links above and it'll be handled promptly.

## Changes to this policy

Any updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
