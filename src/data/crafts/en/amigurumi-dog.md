## Abbreviations

| Abbr. | Meaning                          | Abbr. | Color |
| ----- | -------------------------------- | ----- | ----- |
| ch    | chain                            | cr    | brown |
| sc    | single crochet                   | be    | beige |
| st(s) | stitch(es)                       | wh    | white |
| inc   | increase (2 sc in 1 st)          | bl    | blue  |
| dec   | decrease (2 sts worked together) | rd    | red   |
| hdc   | half double crochet              | bk    | black |

## Body

For the body, use beige (be), blue (bl) and white (wh) yarn.

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: inc x 6 (12)
3. Round 3: [1 sc, inc] x 6 (18)
4. Round 4: [2 sc, inc] x 6 (24)
5. Round 5: [3 sc, inc] x 6 (30)
6. Round 6: [4 sc, inc] x 6 (36)
7. Round 7: [5 sc, inc] x 6 (42)
8. Round 8: [6 sc, inc] x 6 (48)
9. Rounds 9–23: 1 sc in each st (48)
10. Rounds 24–25: switch to cream, 1 sc in each st (48)
11. Rounds 26–27: switch to blue (bl), 1 sc in each st (48)
12. Rounds 28–29: switch to cream, 1 sc in each st (48)
13. Rounds 30–31: switch to blue (bl), 1 sc in each st (48)
14. Rounds 32–33: switch to cream, 1 sc in each st (48)
15. Rounds 34–35: switch to blue (bl), 1 sc in each st (48)
16. Rounds 36–37: switch to cream, 1 sc in each st (48)
17. Round 38: switch to blue (bl), [6 sc, dec] x 6 (42)
18. Round 39: 1 sc in each st (42)
19. Round 40: [5 sc, dec] x 6 (36)
20. Round 41: [4 sc, dec] x 6 (30)
21. Round 42: [3 sc, dec] x 6 (24)
22. Stuff firmly with fiberfill.
23. Round 43: [2 sc, dec] x 6 (18)
24. Round 44: [1 sc, dec] x 6 (12)
25. Round 45: dec x 6 (6)
26. Fasten off and sew the opening closed.

## Tail

For the tail, use beige (be).

1. Round 1: 4 sc into a magic ring (4)
2. Rounds 2–4: 1 sc in each st (4)
3. Round 5: [1 sc, inc] x 2 (6)
4. Rounds 6–9: 1 sc in each st (6)
5. Round 10: [2 sc, inc] x 2 (8)
6. Rounds 11–14: 1 sc in each st (8)
7. Round 15: [3 sc, inc] x 2 (10)
8. Rounds 16–18: 1 sc in each st (10)
9. Stuff lightly, fasten off and sew to the end of the body.

## Back Legs (make 2)

For the back legs, use beige (be).

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: [inc x 2, 1 sc] x 2 (10)
3. Round 3: 2 sc, inc x 2, 3 sc, inc x 2, 1 sc (14)
4. Round 4: 2 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc (18)
5. Round 5: [2 sc, inc] x 6 (24)
6. Round 6: working in the back loops only, 1 sc in each st (24)
7. Round 7: dec x 6, 12 sc (18)
8. Round 8: dec x 3, 12 sc (15)
9. Rounds 9–12: 1 sc in each st (15)
10. Fasten off. Stuff and sew to the body.

## Front Legs (make 2)

For the front legs, use beige (be).

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: [inc x 2, 1 sc] x 2 (10)
3. Round 3: 2 sc, inc x 2, 3 sc, inc x 2, 1 sc (14)
4. Round 4: 2 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 4 sc, inc, 1 sc, inc, 2 sc (18)
5. Round 5: [2 sc, inc] x 6 (24)
6. Round 6: working in the back loops only, 1 sc in each st (24)
7. Round 7: dec x 6, 12 sc (18)
8. Round 8: dec x 3, 12 sc (15)
9. Switch to cream.
10. Rounds 9–10: 1 sc in each st (15)
11. Switch to blue (bl).
12. Rounds 11–12: 1 sc in each st (15)
13. Stuff, fasten off and sew to the body.

## Head

For the head, use beige (be).

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: inc x 6 (12)
3. Round 3: 3 sc, [1 sc, inc] x 4, 1 sc (16)
4. Round 4: 1 sc in each st (16)
5. Round 5: 6 sc, [1 sc, inc] x 5 (21) — the first 6 sc mark the base of the head
6. Round 6: 1 sc in each st (21)
7. Round 7: 6 sc, [2 sc, inc] x 5 (26)
8. Rounds 8–12: 1 sc in each st (26)
9. Round 13: 6 sc, [3 sc, inc] x 5 (31)
10. Rounds 14–16: 1 sc in each st (31)
11. Round 17: 6 sc, [4 sc, inc] x 5 (36)
12. Round 18: 1 sc in each st (36)
13. Round 19: [5 sc, inc] x 6 (42)
14. Round 20: [6 sc, inc] x 6 (48)
15. Round 21: [7 sc, inc] x 6 (54)
16. Round 22: [7 sc, dec] x 6 (48)
17. Round 23: [6 sc, dec] x 6 (42)
18. Round 24: [5 sc, dec] x 6 (36)
19. Round 25: [4 sc, dec] x 6 (30)
20. Round 26: [3 sc, dec] x 6 (24)
21. Round 27: [2 sc, dec] x 6 (18)
22. Stuff firmly with fiberfill.
23. Round 28: [1 sc, dec] x 6 (12)
24. Round 29: dec x 6 (6)
25. Fasten off and sew the opening closed.
26. Sew the head to the body.

## Ears (make 2)

For the ears, use brown (cr).

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: inc x 6 (12)
3. Round 3: [1 sc, inc] x 6 (18)
4. Round 4: [2 sc, inc] x 6 (24)
5. Rounds 5–12: 1 sc in each st (24)
6. Round 13: [6 sc, dec] x 3 (21)
7. Rounds 14–15: 1 sc in each st (21)
8. Round 16: [5 sc, dec] x 3 (18)
9. Rounds 17–18: 1 sc in each st (18)
10. Round 19: [4 sc, dec] x 3 (15)
11. Rounds 20–21: 1 sc in each st (15)
12. Round 22: [3 sc, dec] x 3 (12)
13. Rounds 23–24: 1 sc in each st (12)
14. Fasten off. Sew the opening closed and attach to the head.

## Nose

For the nose, use black (bk).

1. Round 1: 6 sc into a magic ring (6)
2. Round 2: [1 sc, 2 hdc in the next st] x 3 (9)
3. Fasten off and sew to the tip of the muzzle.

## Bow Tie

For the bow tie, use red (rd).

1. Chain 61.
2. Row 1: starting in the second chain from the hook, 60 sc (60)
3. Rows 2–3: ch 1, 60 sc.
4. Fasten off. Weave the ends inside and wrap the bow tie around the neck. Use black (bk) yarn to embroider the eyes.
