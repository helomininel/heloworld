## Ingredients

- 10 tbsp sugar (160 g) — I used 5 tbsp last time
- 3 eggs
- 5 tbsp yogurt (105 g)
- 5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 230 g all-purpose flour (approximately 17 tbsp)
- 16 g baking powder (approximately 1 1/2 tbsp)
- 125 g mixed berries, or any fruit of your choice
- Sliced almonds, for topping
- Vanilla extract, optional

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Oven.** Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Grease a baking dish or line it with parchment paper.
2. **Mix the Eggs and Sugar.** Beat the eggs, add the sugar, and mix well until combined.
3. **Add the Remaining Ingredients.** Add the yogurt, olive oil, flour, baking powder, and vanilla extract, if using. Mix until you have a smooth batter. You can do this entirely by hand.
4. **Fold in the Berries.** Add the berries and gently fold them into the batter. If using frozen berries, there is no need to thaw them first.
5. **Prepare the Cake.** Pour the batter into the prepared baking dish and sprinkle the sliced almonds evenly over the top.
6. **Bake.** Bake for 40–45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.
7. **Cool and Serve.** Allow the cake to cool slightly before slicing and serving.

## Tips

- You can reduce the sugar to 5 tablespoons (80 g) for a less sweet cake.
- Use frozen berries straight from the freezer; thawing is not necessary.
- Try other fruits, such as chopped apples, peaches, or pears.
- Mix the batter by hand to keep the preparation simple.
- You can use a rectangular baking dish or make individual cupcakes instead.
- Check the cake towards the end of the baking time, as ovens and baking dishes can vary.
