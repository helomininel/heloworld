## Ingredients

### For the Dough

- 400 g all-purpose flour
- 8 g salt
- 25 g sugar
- 4 g instant dry yeast
- 45 ml vegetable oil
- 100 ml whole milk, cold or at room temperature
- Up to 115 ml water, preferably cold; add gradually

### For the Filling

- 400–500 g of your favourite prepared filling, cooled completely
- Cream cheese, spinach and cream cheese, or your favourite chicken filling are all great options

### For the Egg Wash

- 1 egg
- A little milk
- A pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. **Mix the Dough.** Combine the flour, salt, sugar, yeast, oil, and milk in a large bowl. Gradually add the water, reserving a little to adjust the consistency.
2. **Knead.** Mix until a dough forms, then knead on the work surface for about 10 minutes, stretching the dough with the heel of your hand and folding it back over itself.
3. **Rest the Dough.** Cover and rest for 5 minutes. If the dough feels too dry, knead in a little of the reserved water. Place it in a sealed plastic bag and refrigerate for 15 minutes.
4. **Fold and Rest Again.** If the dough still tears easily, gently fold it over itself inside the bag. Refrigerate for another 15 minutes.
5. **First Rise.** Shape the dough into a ball and place it in a lightly greased bowl. Turn it over to coat the surface, cover, and leave to rise for 1–2 hours, depending on room temperature.
6. **Divide the Dough.** Gently press out the air and divide the dough into two equal portions. Keep one portion covered or refrigerated while working with the other.
7. **Roll Out.** Flatten one portion into a rectangle, fold it in half, then roll it out to approximately 24 × 28 cm. Roll first across the width, then lengthwise, adjusting the edges as needed.
8. **Add the Filling.** Mark the centre of the rectangle as a guide. Arrange your cooled filling down the middle, leaving the edges free. If using a filling that melts, choose a thick, oven-stable cheese or spread.
9. **Shape the Loaf.** For a striped or braided finish, cut parallel strips along both sides of the dough without cutting into the central filling. Fold the strips over the filling alternately, crossing them over one another. For a simpler finish, fold the dough over the filling, adjust the sides, and seal the ends. Repeat with the second portion.
10. **Prepare the Egg Wash.** Beat the egg with a little milk and a pinch of salt. Brush the shaped loaves lightly.
11. **Second Rise.** Place the loaves on a greased or lined baking tray. Cover and leave to rise for another hour.
12. **Preheat the Oven.** About 30 minutes before the end of the second rise, preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Brush the loaves with another layer of egg wash.
13. **Bake.** Bake at 180 °C for 30–40 minutes, turning the tray halfway through baking for even browning. The loaves should be golden brown and glossy.
14. **Cool and Serve.** Let the bread rest briefly before slicing and serving.

## Tips

- Try different fillings! Plain cream cheese, spinach and cream cheese, ham and cheese, or your favourite chicken filling all work well.
- Everyone has their own favourite filling recipe, so season and prepare it your own way.
- Let the filling cool completely before assembling the bread.
- If using spinach, cook it first and squeeze out excess liquid to prevent soggy bread.
- Use a thick, oven-stable creamy cheese if you want it to hold its shape during baking.
- Avoid overfilling the bread and seal the edges well to prevent leaks.
- The dough can be kneaded in a stand mixer: 5 minutes on low speed, followed by 5–10 minutes on the next speed.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
