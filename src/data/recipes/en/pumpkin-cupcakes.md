## Ingredients

### For the Pumpkin Cupcakes

- 125 g all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground ginger
- 1/8 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/8 tsp ground cloves
- A pinch of black pepper
- 120 ml (1/2 cup) vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 100 g light or dark brown sugar
- 226 g (1 cup) pumpkin purée
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

### For the Cream Cheese Frosting

- 200 g full-fat cream cheese, at room temperature
- 100 g butter, at room temperature
- 100 g sugar
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- A squeeze of lemon juice

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Oven.** Preheat the oven to 175 °C fan-assisted (or 177 °C conventional). Line a 12-hole muffin tin.
2. **Combine the Dry Ingredients.** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and spices until evenly combined.
3. **Mix the Wet Ingredients + Sugar.** In a separate bowl, whisk together the oil, eggs, sugar, pumpkin purée, and vanilla extract until smooth.
4. **Combine.** Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix gently until no dry flour remains.
5. **Bake the Cupcakes.** Divide the batter evenly among the 12 cupcake cases, filling each approximately two-thirds full. Bake for 20–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean. Allow to cool completely before frosting.
6. **Prepare the Cream Cheese Frosting.** Ensure the cream cheese and butter are at room temperature. Beat them together on medium-low speed for 1–2 minutes until smooth, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed.
7. **Add the Sugar and Flavouring.** Sift in the sugar and add the vanilla extract. Beat on low speed until combined, then on medium-high for another 1–2 minutes until light and fluffy. Add a small squeeze of lemon juice and mix briefly.
8. **Frost and Serve.** Once the cupcakes have cooled completely, spread or pipe the frosting over each cupcake. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready to serve.

## Tips

- Use full-fat block cream cheese rather than spreadable cream cheese from a tub for a thicker frosting.
- Make sure the cupcakes are completely cool before frosting to prevent the frosting from melting.
- Store frosted cupcakes in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
- Let the cupcakes sit at room temperature briefly before serving for the best texture.
